Experience the Virtual Bansuri

Our browser-based virtual flute allows you to explore the enchanting sounds of a traditional Bansuri without the need for physical instruments or software installations. Whether you are using a mouse, a touchscreen, or your computer keyboard, playing melodies is just a tap away.

Start your musical journey by familiarizing yourself with the natural notes (Sa, Re, Ga...). Practice basic sequences slowly, focusing on the transition between keys. While this digital tool cannot replicate the complex breath techniques of a real transverse flute, it serves as an excellent companion for understanding note placements, ear training, and composing quick musical ideas.

Beginner's Note Guide

Use this quick reference table to understand how Western notes map to Indian Sargam and your computer keyboard.

Western Note Indian Sargam Keyboard Key Practice Idea
C Sa (Root) A Establish your base pitch.
D Re S Try playing Sa-Re-Sa.
E Ga D Practice stepping up: Sa-Re-Ga.
F Ma F Focus on clean transitions.
G Pa G A strong resting note. Play Sa-Pa.
A Dha H Higher register steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download any app to play this?

No, this is a fully web-based synthesizer. As long as you have a modern web browser and an internet connection, you can play the flute directly on this webpage.

Can I play sharps and flats (Komal/Tivra swaras)?

Yes! Just click the "Sharps and flats" toggle in the control panel to switch the scale. This will allow you to play chromatic notes essential for various Raagas.

Does this teach me real flute fingering?

This tool provides simplified, linear buttons that represent the notes for digital practice. A physical Bansuri requires specific finger hole coverage which cannot be fully replicated on a keyboard.

Can I blow into my device's microphone to play?

Currently, this tool uses touch, mouse clicks, and keyboard strokes to trigger notes. Microphone breath-control is not supported in this version.
