Play BAAZIGAR – Online Indian Taash Card Game

BAAZIGAR is a browser-based desi card game inspired by the traditional Minus Taas Ki Mehfil experience. Play a four-player Taash game with three computer opponents, choose your Hukum, make your Bol, follow the suit and try to win tricks. Start playing directly in your browser without downloading an app.

How to play Minus Taas

Review your cards, select Hukum when prompted and place your Bol. During each trick, follow the lead suit whenever you can. Track the tricks you win and your total score on the live scoreboard. Choose a 31-point or 51-point game and enjoy a classic Indian playing-card experience.

Game features

  • Four-player online-style card game with three AI opponents.
  • Traditional Taash suits: Hukum (Spades), Paan (Hearts), Eent (Diamonds) and Chidi (Clubs).
  • Bol bidding, trick-taking and live score tracking.
  • 31-point and 51-point game modes.
  • Virtual Coins for in-game play; Coins are not real money.
  • Play on desktop, tablet or mobile browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is BAAZIGAR free to play?

Yes. You can open the game in a modern web browser and play without paying real money. Coins shown in the game are virtual game tokens only.

Can I play this Taash game on my mobile?

Yes. The game layout is designed to work on mobile phones as well as desktop browsers.

What is Minus Taas Ki Mehfil?

It is a four-player trick-taking card game experience where players use Hukum, Bol bidding, tricks and scores to compete.

Do I need to download an app?

No app download is required. Open the website in your browser and start the game.
